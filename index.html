A skin for Video.js v10

VIDEOBOY

A video player built like a handheld. Full colour while it plays. The moment it stops, the screen becomes a four-tone dot-matrix panel.

Press A or Space to start. Pause any time and watch the screen change.

Theme

01 · The idea

The screen is the skin.

Most players lay a strip of controls over the picture and leave it there. VIDEOBOY moves them off the picture entirely. The film sits in a screen bezel, and the controls live on the console below it, where a handheld keeps them. Nothing covers the picture while it plays.

The handheld’s best-known part is its screen, so that is what carries the idea. Pause, and the frame pixelates, then snaps to four tones of a reflective LCD. Every stop gets its own screen: a boot logo, a start menu, a pause window, an end card, and static when the signal drops.

02 · Anatomy

Every part of the handheld is a real control.

Nothing on the console is decoration. Each part is a Video.js v10 component, and each one shows its state on its face.

    05 · Sizes

    One handheld, four layouts.

    The layout follows the player’s own width through container queries, not the window’s. Wide players keep one row. Narrow ones stack the seek strip on top and stand up like the handheld itself. The bezel, labels and keys scale with it; touch targets never drop below 40 px for keys and 24 px for D-pad arms.

      07 · Accessibility

      Checked, not assumed.

      • 0axe-core violations in 78 runs: WCAG 2.2 AA and best-practice rules, three themes, at 1100, 640 and 360 px, in eight states from boot to error, plus this page.
      • 3 pxFocus ring on every control, offset from the key so it reads on any shell colour.
      • NamesEvery key is a Video.js button with its own name and shortcut. The LCD is drawn for the eyes and hidden from screen readers; the status announcer speaks play, pause, seek and volume changes.
      • CaptionsSet in the system sans on dark plates, never in the pixel font, sized to the player.
      • MotionWith reduced motion, the pause screen appears without the mosaic, nothing blinks, and the logo does not drop.
      • ContrastLabels and timecodes reach at least 6.6:1 on their shell, LCD text at least 6.6:1, in all three themes. Key icons reach at least 5.7:1.
      • Forced coloursIn Windows high contrast the handheld redraws itself in system colours, with outlined keys and a Highlight seek fill.
      • TargetsA, B and the system keys are 40 px or more; D-pad arms are at least 24 px; the seek strip and grille are 44 px tall to grab.

      08 · Install

      Two imports and one element.

      Download the skin source (zip) Put its src folder in your project, then:

      # Video.js v10 and the Mux media element
npm i @videojs/html @videojs/mux-video
      import './videoboy/skin';
import './videoboy/videoboy.css';
import '@videojs/html/media/mux-video';
      <video-player>
  <videoboy-skin data-theme="tangerine" poster-time="36">
    <mux-video playback-id="YOUR_PLAYBACK_ID" playsinline crossorigin="anonymous"></mux-video>
  </videoboy-skin>
</video-player>

      Any <video> works in place of <mux-video>. Cross-origin footage without CORS still plays; its pause screen falls back to an SVG tone filter instead of dots. The skin’s source is Apache-2.0.
